1. Sales & Revenue Overview
🎯 Audience: Management
📈 Visuals:

Total Bookings & Revenue (KPI cards)

Revenue Trend (Line chart by month/year)

Bookings by Channel (Stacked column chart)

Revenue by Package Type or Destination (Bar chart or Tree map)

🔍 Key Questions:

What is the overall trend in bookings and revenue?

Which channels generate the most bookings?

Which destinations or packages are the most popular and profitable?

How has revenue changed over time?

2. Customer Insights
🎯 Audience: Marketing & CRM
📈 Visuals:

Customer Age Distribution (Histogram)

Customer Type Split (Pie chart)

Gender Split (Donut chart)

Repeat vs. New Customers Over Time (Line chart or stacked bar)

🔍 Key Questions:

What are the demographics of our customers?

Are most customers new or repeat?

What customer type spends the most or books more frequently?

Are there any trends by gender or age?

3. Package Performance
🎯 Audience: Product/Package Team
📈 Visuals:

Most Booked Packages (Bar chart)

Avg Price per Pax by Category (Box plot or bar chart)

Duration vs. Price (Scatter plot)

Package Booking Heatmap (Calendar or matrix view)

🔍 Key Questions:

Which packages are most/least popular?

Is there a trend in pricing vs. duration?

Which destinations attract the most customers?

Are certain package categories doing better than others?

4. Guide Performance
🎯 Audience: Operations / HR
📈 Visuals:

Average Rating by Guide (Bar chart)

Booking count by Guide (Bar chart)

Guide Commission Payouts (Table or bar chart)

Working vs. Non-working Status (Pie chart)

🔍 Key Questions:

Which guides receive the best/worst ratings?

Who handles the most bookings?

What are the commission payouts across guides?

How many guides are currently active?

5. Customer Feedback Analysis
🎯 Audience: Customer Experience / Quality Assurance
📈 Visuals:

Average Satisfaction Score (KPI + Trend)

Ratings Breakdown (Hotel vs Guide) – Clustered Bar

Word Cloud for Comments (Text analytics)

% Would Recommend (Gauge or KPI)

🔍 Key Questions:

What is the average satisfaction score over time?

How do hotel and guide ratings compare?

What are common themes in feedback?

What percentage of customers would recommend us?

6. Refund Analysis
🎯 Audience: Finance / Customer Service
📈 Visuals:

Refund Rate Trend (Line or bar chart)

Refunds by Reason (Bar chart or Tree map)

Avg Refund Amount by Package (Bar chart)

Refund Status Distribution (Pie chart)

🔍 Key Questions:

How often do refunds occur, and is it increasing?

What are the main reasons for refunds?

Which packages have the highest refund amounts or rates?

What is the status of most refunds (completed/pending)?

7. Time Intelligence
🎯 Audience: Analysts
📈 Visuals:

Bookings by Month & Year (Heatmap or Matrix)

Booking vs Travel Lag (Histogram or Scatter)

Weekend vs Weekday Bookings (Stacked bar)

🔍 Key Questions:

Which months or weekdays are peak booking periods?

How far in advance do customers book?

Is there a seasonality trend?

🚀 BONUS: Actionable Metrics
Churn Risk: Combine low satisfaction + refund behavior

High-Value Customers: Customers with multiple bookings and high spend

Underperforming Packages: High refund + low satisfaction
